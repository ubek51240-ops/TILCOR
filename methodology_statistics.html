2. Formulalar va 2×2 Kontingentlik Hisoblagichi

Kollokatsiya assotsiatsiya mezonlari: Pointwise Mutual Information ($MI$), Student's $t$-score va Dunning Log-Likelihood ($G^2$)

Pointwise Mutual Information (MI):

$$MI = \log_2 \left( \frac{O_{11} \cdot N}{R_1 \cdot C_1} \right)$$

Student's t-score:

$$t = \frac{O_{11} - E_{11}}{\sqrt{O_{11}}}, \quad \text{bu yerda } E_{11} = \frac{R_1 \cdot C_1}{N}$$

Dunning Log-Likelihood (G², 1993):

$$G^2 = 2 \sum_{i=1}^2 \sum_{j=1}^2 O_{ij} \ln \left( \frac{O_{ij}}{E_{ij}} \right)$$

Akademik izoh (1.1-fasl): $MI$ ko‘rsatkichi kam uchraydigan noyob juftliklarga yuqori ball berishga moyil bo‘lsa, $t$-score esa yuqori chastotali grammatik kollokatsiyalarni afzal ko‘radi. Shu sababli Dunning $G^2$ ko‘rsatkichi kichik korpuslar uchun eng mustahkam mezon hisoblanadi.
